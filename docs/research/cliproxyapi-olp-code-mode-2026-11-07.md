# CLIProxyAPI and OLP code-mode boundaries

Inspected November 7, 2026. The source baselines are [CLIProxyAPI c5251b57b3fa5dcbc689f514f4aa166daacb4481](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/tree/c5251b57b3fa5dcbc689f514f4aa166daacb4481) and [OLP 0ab1118c](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/tree/0ab1118c). This comparison records those implementations and the separately approved [#679 direction](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/issues/679). It does not claim the initiative was already implemented at the baseline.

## Selection and allowance

CLIProxyAPI's [passive observer](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/blob/c5251b57b3fa5dcbc689f514f4aa166daacb4481/sdk/cliproxy/auth/quota_signals.go#L26-L54) updates `ObservedAt` and `Signals` without changing scheduling or cooldown. Its [selector](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/blob/c5251b57b3fa5dcbc689f514f4aa166daacb4481/sdk/cliproxy/auth/selector.go) applies credential and model availability, priority and the configured selection strategy. Passive remaining percentages do not rank ready accounts. Its availability checks distinguish credential-wide quota from individual model state, and an established affinity can outrank priority while its credential remains available.

At the OLP baseline, [availability SQL](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/blob/0ab1118c/internal/database/migrations/0139_code_allowance_freshness.sql) lets exhausted percent windows and top-level count observations exclude accounts. The [selection path](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/blob/0ab1118c/internal/codesessions/store.go) leaves model-family association empty, disabling effective allowance ordering. [Offers](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/blob/0ab1118c/internal/codesessions/offers.go) filter model cooldowns, while the [management projection](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/blob/0ab1118c/internal/providers/code_mode.go) checks account availability without considering whether any model remains outside cooldown. Those differences can make an exhausted, unrelated meter hide usable capacity.

The #680 change removes admission and refusal deadlines derived solely from unproven allowance scope. Explicit account health, current authorization and classified cooldowns still apply. Management eligibility additionally requires an enrolled model outside all applicable cooldowns. #681 owns exact-model advisory ordering, with healthy, unknown, low and exhausted bands and weights within bands. A matched exhausted observation can lower preference without excluding the account.

The first-party [Codex client at d4a341bd52cc47ff635761334bde8a972d6de943](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/d4a341bd52cc47ff635761334bde8a972d6de943/codex-rs/tui/src/chatwidget/rate_limits.rs#L193-L210) compares an additional window's `limit_name` to the selected model when deciding refresh cadence. This establishes a model association useful for advisory ordering. It does not prove that the window's meter identifier equals a model name, that an exhausted window prevents execution, or that its scope is account-wide. Unsupported associations remain unknown and active readers remain disabled pending qualification.

## Recovery and compatibility

CLIProxyAPI's [execution loop](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/blob/c5251b57b3fa5dcbc689f514f4aa166daacb4481/sdk/cliproxy/auth/conductor_execution.go) retries according to configured retry settings and attempted-credential tracking. Its [result handling](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/blob/c5251b57b3fa5dcbc689f514f4aa166daacb4481/sdk/cliproxy/auth/conductor_cooldown.go#L888) records model or credential-scoped cooldown based on execution outcome. Those retry mechanisms are not evidence of OLP's stronger proof-of-non-execution contract.

OLP's baseline [recovery path](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/blob/0ab1118c/internal/codesessions/recovery.go) and [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-state-aware-code-account-recovery.md) require known non-execution and portable state before moving work. Accepted work, partial output and uncertain dispatch do not replay. #679 retains this boundary while adding explicitly published compatibility policies under [ADR 0001](../adr/0001-explicit-code-mode-compatibility.md). It does not authorize generic pre-output retry, implicit paid fallback or moving account-owned state.

The [Responses API reference](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/python/resources/responses/methods/create), checked November 7, 2026, documents a minimum output cap of 16. #679 deliberately applies that minimum to newly published translated Chat and Messages requests targeting Codex and accounts for the effective cap per attempt. The separate subscription endpoint's equivalence is live-unverified. Native forwarding and existing publications keep their contracts until deliberate republication.

Signed Gemini continuations and opt-in client-held encrypted summary compaction are initiative decisions in [ADR 0006](../adr/0006-model-aware-code-mode-boundaries.md), not #680 runtime capabilities. They require account and conversation ownership, durable state or keys before successful completion, normal usage accounting and retirement cleanup. Grok identity compatibility likewise requires a separate policy and verified enrollment identity. Source inspection of another gateway cannot qualify these guarantees for OLP.

## Evidence and qualification

Source implementation evidence shows what a pinned tree does. The directly checked upstream files above support the selection, observation and cooldown comparison. The [earlier quota survey](cliproxyapi-quota-signals.md) retains its own November 5 date and different pinned revisions. Its vendor and plan observations must not silently acquire this document's newer baseline.

Fixture qualification shows project-owned behavior against controlled peers and storage. #680 extends the existing `tests/service/code_journey_test.go` journey for fresh, stale, resetless, unrelated and count observations, plus real-binary management eligibility alongside model offers and admission under cooldowns. These fixtures prove that local observations cannot silently become global admission authority. They do not prove vendor charging scope or service acceptance. The parent requires six separately identified real-client fixture lanes for the broader adapter work. Those lanes are not supplied or passed by #680.

Live qualification requires an authorized end-user workflow against the actual subscription service, with the client version, platform, adapter, profile, model, protocol and transport recorded. No live qualification was performed for #680. Neither the pinned implementations nor service fixtures establish provider acceptance of shared subscriptions, translated output minimums, independent client identities or compaction. Terms and account suspension disclosures remain necessary, and fixture success must not be presented as a live reliability claim.
