# Model-aware allowance and published compatibility boundaries

Accepted under [#679](https://github.com/tyk-swe/olp/issues/679). This decision supersedes ADR 0005's exclusion of exhausted observations and its requirement for proven charging scope before advisory ordering. It retains [ADR 0001](0001-explicit-code-mode-compatibility.md)'s explicit published compatibility and [ADR 0002](0002-state-aware-code-account-recovery.md)'s proof of non-execution before recovery.

A fresh passive Codex window whose model-facing limit name exactly and unambiguously matches the requested model is advisory evidence. It orders healthy, unknown, low and exhausted accounts, preserving weights within each band. A meter identifier is distinct from a model-facing name. Missing, stale, ambiguous, unrelated or exhausted observations cannot establish account-wide exclusion, health or cooldown. Explicit account health, authorization, supported models and classified model or credential cooldowns remain admission authority. Management eligibility means at least one enrolled model remains usable, while route offers and admission apply the requested model. Affinity does not override these restrictions. Unsupported mappings remain unknown and active allowance readers remain disabled until qualified.

For newly published translated Chat and Messages requests to Codex, a positive output cap below 16 becomes 16. The caller may receive more output than requested. This follows the public Responses API minimum as a compatibility choice, and equivalence at the subscription endpoint remains live-unverified. Reservations must use the selected target's effective cap before every attempt, including recovery and fallback. Native requests, other targets and the existing refusal of unsupported hard token guarantees keep their contracts.

Each additional compatibility surface, Grok client identity behavior and summary compaction has its own default-off policy in an immutable route publication. Existing revisions retain their behavior until republished. Grok enrollment and refresh require verified provider identity and principal continuity. Provider terms and account suspension risk require disclosure, without a new acknowledgement gate or an inference of provider acceptance.

Signed Gemini continuation state belongs to its root conversation and originating account. Store the minimum association encrypted and durably before successful client completion, retain it through restart and remove it on retirement. Persistence failure after dispatch cannot authorize replay. Refuse unresolved references and foreign state rather than discarding them.

Opt-in summary compaction makes an additional normally accounted provider call and may lose detail. Require complete representable history and return an authenticated encrypted client-held summary bound to account and conversation. Retain only its conversation-specific encryption key through existing encrypted secret management, surviving restart and secret rotation until retirement. Diagnostics retain no prompts, outputs, summaries, tool payloads, raw headers, credentials or signed state.

These boundaries govern the initiative, rather than claiming every sibling capability is implemented by #680. The [dated comparison](../research/cliproxyapi-olp-code-mode-2026-11-07.md) separates source implementation, fixture evidence and live qualification.
